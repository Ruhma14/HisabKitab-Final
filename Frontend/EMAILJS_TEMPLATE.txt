EMAILJS TEMPLATE BODY
=====================

Use this as the EmailJS template content. Keep the EmailJS "To Email" field set to:
{{recipient_email}}

Email body:

Dear {{to_name}},

Your transaction with Alam Garments has been recorded successfully.

Transaction Details:
Type: {{transaction_type}}
Amount: Rs. {{amount}}
Date: {{date}}
Reference: {{bill_number}}
Previous Balance: {{previous_balance}}
Current Balance: {{current_balance}}
Description: {{description}}

Thank you for doing business with Alam Garments.

Regards,
Alam Garments

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT write "Rs." before {{previous_balance}} or {{current_balance}}, because those variables already contain "Rs.".
- Do NOT write "Rs. Rs." before {{amount}}. The template above uses numeric {{amount}} and adds Rs. once.
- Keep To Email as {{recipient_email}}.
