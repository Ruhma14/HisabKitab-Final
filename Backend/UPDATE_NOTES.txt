HisabKitab Backend - Supplier Balance Fix

This backend version fixes supplier financial calculations using the supplier ledger.

Key fix:
- Opening Balance is treated as brought-forward payable.
- Purchases increase supplier payable by the unpaid portion.
- Purchase payments and supplier payments reduce payable.
- Purchase returns reduce payable.
- Existing suppliers are recalculated from their ledger when suppliers/bootstrap/dashboard/reports are loaded.
- Existing records with an openingBalance but stale currentBalance are automatically corrected.
- Supplier payment validation now checks the recalculated real outstanding balance.

Example:
Opening Balance: Rs. 90,000
Purchase: Rs. 6,600 paid in full
Outstanding: Rs. 90,000
Payment: Rs. 4,500
New Outstanding: Rs. 85,500
